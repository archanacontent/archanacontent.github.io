Channel Engine

A private publishing tool for five YouTube channels owned by one creator.

Channel Engine renders short educational videos and publishes them to the YouTube channels it was built for: AI Search Marketing, Marketing Automation Library, Cybersecurity Daily, Technical Writing For Beginners and Space Science. Every video is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before it goes public.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their visibility, add them to playlists and attach caption files. It acts only on channels that the owner has authorised, and it is not offered to the public or to other YouTube users.

Read the privacy policy and terms of service for how YouTube data is handled.
