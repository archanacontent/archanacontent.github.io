Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Channel Engine is a private tool that publishes videos to YouTube channels owned by its operator. It uses YouTube API Services. By using Channel Engine, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

When a channel owner authorises Channel Engine, it receives an access token for that channel through Google's OAuth sign-in. With it, the tool reads the channel's name and handle, its playlists, and the videos it has uploaded, so it can confirm it is publishing to the right channel and place each video in the right playlist.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's own videos, change their visibility from private to public after approval, add them to playlists and attach caption files. Channel Engine does not read comments, subscriber lists, viewer data or any information about other YouTube users.

How the data is stored

Authorisation tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository controlled by the channel owner. Video files and metadata are created by the tool itself. No YouTube data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

These pages and the tool set no cookies and collect no analytics.

Revoking access and deleting data

A channel owner can revoke Channel Engine's access at any time from the Google security settings page. Revoking access makes the stored token unusable, and the owner can delete it from the repository's secrets. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact address below and are completed within 7 days.
